# AI-Assisted Contributions Policy

AI tools can be useful, but they do not transfer responsibility away from the person contributing the work.

## Scope

This policy applies to all contributions to PSLDiscordBot and PSLBotMiscPlugins, including code, configuration, documentation, issue and pull-request content, reviews, and assets. It applies equally to maintainers, team members, and external contributors.

"AI-assisted" includes work created or modified with large language models, generative AI, coding assistants, agentic tools, or machine-translation tools.

## Requirements

AI-assisted contributions are permitted when the contributor:

- Personally reviews and understands every AI-assisted part of the contribution, can explain it, and takes full responsibility for it.
- Checks its accuracy, security, quality, and suitability to the same standard as any other contribution.
- Performs and accurately reports the validation appropriate for the work. Never claim that a build, test, reproduction, source check, or review occurred when it did not.
- Has the right to submit all included material and checks for copied or incompatibly licensed content.
- Follows all other project policies and contribution requirements.

Never provide an AI tool or service with credentials, secrets, personal or private user data, private reports or conversations, or any other material you do not have permission to share with that tool or its provider.

## Disclosure and review

Routine disclosure of AI use is not required. However, a maintainer may ask when it is relevant to reviewing a contribution. The contributor must then answer accurately and identify:

- The tool and model used, if known.
- Which parts of the contribution it assisted with or produced.
- How those parts were reviewed and validated.

"The AI produced it" is not a substitute for an explanation or a correction.

## Automated and bulk submissions

Using an interactive AI tool under active human supervision is allowed. Unattended AI agents, AI-operated accounts, and bulk or campaign-style AI-generated issues or pull requests require maintainer approval before submission.

## Translated text

AI or machine translation may be used to prepare a draft. Before translated text is contributed, a person fluent in the target language must thoroughly review it, correct it as needed, validate its meaning and terminology, and take responsibility for the final wording. Raw machine-generated translation is not acceptable.

## Visual, audio, and other media assets

Submitted images, artwork, designs, audio, video, and similar media must not contain generative-AI-created or generative-AI-replaced content. Non-generative AI-assisted editing, such as denoising, selection or masking, and upscaling, is allowed when it does not create or replace expressive content and the contribution otherwise meets this policy.

## Noncompliance

Maintainers may ask questions, request additional validation, or require corrections. Contributors will normally have an opportunity to address a concern. A contribution that remains noncompliant or cannot be reviewed with sufficient confidence may be rejected or closed.
